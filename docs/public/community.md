# Pull Up Community Guidelines

Last updated: **October 24, 2026**

Pull Up is for sharing vehicles, journeys, builds and meets while respecting other people. These rules apply to profiles, handles, bios, photos, videos, vehicle content, posts, comments, meets, messages and shared cards.

## Keep driving safe

- Do not organize or promote public-road racing, speed challenges, dangerous stunts, impaired driving or unlawful takeovers.
- Do not pressure people to drive faster, farther, longer, or while fatigued to gain a ranking or reward.
- A public road is not a closed course because a post calls it a track. Follow venue rules at legitimate closed-course events.
- Configure and interact with the app while safely parked. Do not encourage filming or operating a phone while driving.

## Respect other people

No threats, harassment, bullying, hate speech, stalking, doxxing or impersonation. Do not publish someone's address, private route, location, messages or identifying details without appropriate permission. Do not evade a block or suspension.

## Keep content appropriate

No sexual exploitation, explicit sexual content, sexualized minors, graphic injury/gore, sadistic violence, or promotion of violence. Do not use Pull Up to sell illegal or controlled goods, operate gambling, distribute scams, or promote drug misuse. Ordinary vehicle tools are not a reason to post threats or weapon-focused content.

Occasional non-targeted profanity or incidental adult references do not excuse harassment, sexual content, or other violations. Do not assume an age rating allows content prohibited by these rules.

## Share only what you may share

Use photos, videos, music and text you own or have permission to publish. Obtain appropriate permission from people shown or whose private material you share. Do not upload copyrighted media, misleading claims of sponsorship, stolen parts offers, or another person's work as your own.

Do not post spam, manipulated engagement, deceptive events, false affiliations, or fake safety/fitment guarantees. Hosts must accurately describe events and have the permissions needed to use the venue.

## Reports and enforcement

Report specific content using its in-app report option. Block a person to restrict interactions. Long-press comments for their reporting options. Do not repost prohibited content publicly to draw attention to a report.

Violations may lead to content removal, feature restrictions, or account suspension/removal. Repeated or serious abuse may lead to permanent restrictions. Enforcement and any review of a disputed decision must be handled by the service operator; no automated check guarantees that every violation will be caught.

For a general support question, [open a public support issue](https://github.com/AaravJit/pull-up/issues/new) without private data. Report emergencies to local emergency services, not Pull Up.
