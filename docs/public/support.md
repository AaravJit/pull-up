# Pull Up Support

Pull Up is developed by **Aarav Jit**. The first App Store release is in preparation.

## Contact the developer

For a general question, reproducible bug, or feature request, [open a support issue](https://github.com/AaravJit/pull-up/issues/new) in the public Pull Up project. You can also [review existing issues](https://github.com/AaravJit/pull-up/issues) and find the [developer's public GitHub profile](https://github.com/AaravJit).

**The issue tracker is public.** Do not post passwords, sign-in codes, Apple credentials, phone numbers, private messages, precise locations, route exports, payment details, or identity documents. Crop or redact screenshots before attaching them. A useful bug report includes the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, and what happened, without private data.

## Sign-in

New public accounts use Sign in with Apple. Email sign-in under **Other ways to sign in** is for accounts already created by Pull Up support, including review accounts. A guest vehicle means recording without a garage vehicle; it does not bypass account sign-in.

## Recording a drive

On **Drive**, tap **Start drive**, select a vehicle or guest vehicle, and confirm recording. The app needs location access for drive recording. If access was denied, review Pull Up's permission in iOS Settings. Precise location is needed for recording accuracy.

Keep the phone secured and follow traffic laws. Configure, start, stop, and review a drive while safely parked. Readings are GPS estimates, not certified measurements or proof of safe or legal driving. Signal loss, interruptions, and operating-system behavior may affect a recording.

## Privacy and deletion

Open **Feed > your profile icon > Settings** to review audience controls, route sharing, protected places, messages, and notification preferences.

To delete your account, use **Settings > Account > Delete account** in Pull Up. Account deletion is different from deleting the app or revoking its permissions. An Apple-account deletion flow may ask you to authenticate again to revoke the app's Apple authorization. If Apple authorization could not be revoked automatically, the app provides instructions to remove it in Apple Account settings.

See the [privacy policy](privacy.md) for storage, sharing, and retention information. For a general deletion problem, open an issue describing only the problem; do not include account credentials or personal data.

## Report content or a person

Use the report/block options on a post, profile, meet, or conversation. Long-press a comment for its options. Report the specific content when possible so the report can include the relevant evidence. Do not copy private messages or personal information into the public issue tracker.

Pull Up is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger.

## Parts searches

**Garage > vehicle > Find parts** builds a search and opens eBay. Confirm fitment, condition, seller, shipping, and purchase terms there. A search result is not a Pull Up fitment guarantee or endorsement.

## Purchases

Pull Up Pro is not offered in the initial release. Do not send payment or accept an unsolicited request for Apple sign-in credentials through a support issue.
