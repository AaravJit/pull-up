# Pull Up Privacy Policy

Last updated: **October 27, 2026**

Pull Up is developed and operated by **Aarav Jit**. This policy describes the initial iPhone service: drive recording, a vehicle garage, meets, a community feed, and one-to-one messages. The initial release does not offer Pull Up Pro purchases or CarPlay.

## Information you provide

- **Account and profile:** Sign in with Apple supplies an account identifier and may supply your name and email, including an Apple private-relay email. We store your username, display name, optional bio and photo, profile presentation, and privacy settings. Pre-created support and review accounts may use email and password sign-in through the authentication provider.
- **Vehicles and community content:** Vehicle specifications, names, photos and modifications; posts (photos, and videos including their sound), comments, likes and follows; meet details, venue coordinates, RSVPs and attendance-display choices; and other content you submit.
- **People you follow and block:** Your follows, follow requests and blocks are stored so the service can apply audience rules, build your Following feed and keep blocked people away from you. Pull Up does not read your phone's address book.
- **Messages:** Text, photos, reactions, shared content references, delivery and read state, and conversation preferences needed to provide messaging. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.
- **Searches:** When you search for people, the name you type is sent to the service to find matching usernames and is not kept. Parts searches are not sent to Pull Up; they open eBay.
- **Reports and support:** Content you report, your report reason and note, and a copy of the reported content taken when you report it. Information you voluntarily provide when seeking support goes to the public GitHub tracker, where other people can see it.

## Drive and location information

When you confirm a drive recording, Pull Up requests location permission and uses location samples to calculate your route, speed, distance, elapsed and moving time, elevation, and related statistics. Recording can continue while the app is backgrounded or the phone is locked. The app requests When In Use authorization, not Always authorization. While the app is open, maps where you place a meet or a protected place can also show your current position; that position is not uploaded unless you choose it as the location.

Recorded samples and pending drives may be saved on your phone for recovery or upload. Recorded drives, their raw GPS tracks and the statistics calculated from them are uploaded to your account when the connection and service permit it. The raw track is visible only to you. Protected-place coordinates and sharing preferences are stored to apply your privacy choices. Missing or poor-quality signals can affect the data.

## Notifications

If you allow push notifications, the service stores a push token and notification preferences to deliver notifications through Apple. Message text previews are off by default. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

## Technical processing and logs

Network requests necessarily expose technical information to the services handling them. Our hosting provider, Supabase, keeps operational logs of requests to the service. Its API request logs record the requesting IP address and request metadata, such as the address requested, and can be looked up by account. Its authentication logs record sign-in and account events with your account identifier, IP address and device details. These logs are kept for the period set by the provider for our hosting plan and are not removed when you delete your account. Pull Up does not use IP addresses to work out where you are.

The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting software and does not collect diagnostics of its own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide crash reports to the developer under Apple's terms.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts; save and synchronize garages and drives; calculate driving statistics; show content according to audience choices; provide meets, feed and messaging; deliver requested notifications; apply privacy zones and shared-route trimming; investigate reports, abuse and technical problems; and respond to support or legal requests. The feed is shown newest first. It is not ranked by your activity.

We do not sell personal information or use the initial native app's data for cross-app advertising tracking. The service is not designed to display other users' live positions.

## What other people can see

Drive history starts private, route sharing starts off, leaderboard participation starts off, and displaying your name in a meet's attendee list is optional. **Profiles initially default to public, new vehicles to friends, posts to public, and message requests to everyone.** Read receipts initially default on. Review these controls during onboarding and in Settings; the whole account is not private by default.

Even when your profile is private, signed-in users can find you by username and see your username, display name and profile photo. If you join a leaderboard, your username, vehicle and totals appear on the boards you qualify for. Meets start public, cannot be made private, and show the venue location you choose.

Public content can be seen by other users. Friends-only visibility depends on the service's relationship rules. People you message receive the content you send. Report evidence is available to authorized service operators for handling the report.

People who can see a drive see its statistics, including its speeds. Sharing a drive and sharing its route are separate choices. A shared route is a redacted copy with endpoint trimming and protected-place removal. Your own raw track remains in your account. Redaction reduces disclosure; it cannot guarantee anonymity or prevent a recipient from inferring information from the rest of your content. Other people may retain screenshots or copies of content already shared with them.

## Service providers and external destinations

- **Supabase** provides backend database, authentication, storage, realtime messaging and server functions. Data is processed to operate these services under the applicable provider arrangements. See [Supabase privacy information](https://supabase.com/privacy).
- **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple, push delivery, maps and operating-system services. Use of those services is also subject to [Apple's privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **eBay:** Only when you choose to open a parts search, your search terms are placed in an external eBay link. That destination may process information under [eBay's privacy notice](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-privacy-notice-privacy-policy?id=4260). The link carries no information about your Pull Up account. A search does not establish fitment or a purchase relationship with Pull Up. Any future affiliate links must be disclosed before opening them.
- **GitHub** hosts this public project, documentation and support tracker. Visiting it or filing an issue is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Moderation alerts:** When someone files a report, the service can send an alert to the developer's chosen notification channel. The alert carries report and content identifiers, the reason and counts only, not the reported content, names or account identifiers.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with a valid legal obligation, protect users or the service, or investigate abuse. We do not authorize service providers to use personal data for unrelated purposes. Provider agreements and safeguards must support the protections described here before production launch.

## Storage, retention and deletion

Account data, garages and saved drives are retained to provide the service until deleted or no longer needed. Raw and pending drive files may remain on your device until successfully synchronized or removed through the app's relevant controls. Signing out is not the same as erasing all account data: drives that have not uploaded stay on this iPhone for that account. Photos the app shows are kept in memory only, not saved to the phone's storage, and are cleared when you sign out.

Use **Feed > your profile icon > Settings > Account > Delete account** to delete your account. Pull Up deletes your profile, garage, drives and GPS tracks, routes, posts, comments, likes, meets you host, follows, notifications, push tokens and the photos and videos you uploaded. It also deletes your conversations, including the messages and photos the other person sent in them. If you use Sign in with Apple, Pull Up also asks Apple to revoke its access to your Apple Account; if that can't be done, the app tells you how to remove it in Settings. If part of the deletion fails, for example because a server is briefly unavailable, the app tells you, and trying again continues where it stopped. On this iPhone, deleting your account also removes its unsent drives, exports and cached photos and signs you out. This action cannot remove copies another person previously saved outside Pull Up.

Reports are kept as evidence for handling abuse. When an account is deleted, the reports it filed or that concern it, and the copies of reported content they hold, are kept without that account's identifier. A record of moderation actions is kept for two years. Media removed by a moderator is held for 30 days as evidence and then deleted.

The messaging-retention design removes unanswered requests after 30 days, content both participants have cleared, and unsent-message tombstones after 30 days. Closed reports are removed after one year; open reports remain while they are handled. Photos and videos from posts you did not finish publishing are removed by a server cleanup. These scheduled cleanups require the deployed retention jobs to run.

Database backups kept by our hosting provider are not edited when an account is deleted; they expire on the provider's schedule for our plan. Stored photos and videos are not part of those backups. Operational logs also have their own retention, described above. We do not claim a fixed backup-deletion deadline that has not been established with the deployed providers. Necessary records may also be retained to meet legal obligations or handle abuse.

## Your choices

You can edit profile and audience settings, disable route sharing, change protected places, opt out of leaderboards, control who may message you, change read receipts and push preferences, revoke iOS permissions, remove content using available controls, and delete your account. Revoking a permission does not by itself delete data already stored. Pull Up still works without location permission, except for recording drives.

The app re-encodes photos and prepares videos to remove location and device metadata before upload. A video keeps its sound. Visible landmarks, sounds and information you type or show can still disclose a location. Do not upload sensitive material you do not intend to share with the chosen audience.

## Children and age

Pull Up is not intended for children under 13. A store age rating is not age verification. Age-related restrictions and regional requirements must be reflected in the app's actual onboarding and controls before public launch. Do not submit a child's personal information through the public support tracker.

## Security

The app uses access controls and operating-system protections, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Authorized operators may access information when needed to operate, secure and support the service or handle reports. Do not send sensitive information assuming messages are end-to-end encrypted.

## Contact and changes

For a general privacy question, [contact the developer through the public support tracker](https://github.com/AaravJit/pull-up/issues/new). Post only a non-sensitive description; do not include credentials, precise locations, private messages or identity documents. Account deletion is available inside the app as described above.

We may update this policy when the service changes and will update the date above. Material changes to collection, use or sharing require corresponding updates to app disclosures and any necessary user choices before the changed processing begins.
