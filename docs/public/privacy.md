# Pull Up Privacy Policy

Last updated: **October 24, 2026**

Pull Up is developed and operated by **Aarav Jit**. This policy describes the initial iPhone service: drive recording, a vehicle garage, meets, a community feed, and one-to-one messages. The initial release does not offer Pull Up Pro purchases or CarPlay.

## Information you provide

- **Account and profile:** Sign in with Apple supplies an account identifier and may supply your name and email, including an Apple private-relay email. We store your username, display name, optional bio/photo, profile presentation, and privacy settings. Pre-created support/review accounts may use email and password sign-in through the authentication provider.
- **Vehicles and community content:** Vehicle specifications, names, photos and modifications; posts, comments, likes and follows; meet details, venue coordinates, RSVPs and attendance-display choices; and other content you submit.
- **Messages:** Text, photos, reactions, shared content references, delivery/read state and conversation preferences needed to provide messaging. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.
- **Reports and support:** Content you report, your report reason and note, relevant content snapshots, and information you voluntarily provide when seeking support. Public GitHub support issues are visible to other people.

## Drive and location information

When you confirm a drive recording, Pull Up requests location permission and uses location samples to calculate your route, speed, distance, elapsed and moving time, elevation, and related statistics. Recording can continue while the app is backgrounded or the phone is locked. The app requests When In Use authorization, not Always authorization.

Recorded samples and pending drives may be saved on your phone for recovery or upload. Recorded drives and their tracks are uploaded to your account when the connection and service permit it. Protected-place coordinates and sharing preferences are stored to apply your privacy choices. Missing or poor-quality signals can affect the data.

## Notifications and technical processing

If you allow push notifications, the service stores a push token and notification preferences to deliver notifications through Apple. Message text previews are off by default. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Network requests necessarily expose technical information such as IP addresses to the services handling them. Our hosting/authentication/storage providers may maintain operational and security logs. The initial native app does not include a separate advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Provider processing and retention must also be considered when assessing the service's privacy practices; this policy does not promise that providers produce no logs.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts; save and synchronize garages and drives; calculate driving statistics; show content according to audience choices; provide meets, feed and messaging; deliver requested notifications; apply privacy zones and shared-route trimming; investigate reports, abuse and technical problems; and respond to support or legal requests.

We do not sell personal information or use the initial native app's data for cross-app advertising tracking. The service is not designed to display other users' live positions.

## What other people can see

Drive history starts private, route sharing starts off, leaderboard participation starts off, and displaying your name in a meet's attendee list is optional. **Profiles initially default to public, new vehicles to friends, and message requests to everyone.** Read receipts initially default on. Review these controls during onboarding and in Settings; the whole account is not private by default.

Public content can be seen by other users. Friends-only visibility depends on the service's relationship rules. People you message receive the content you send. Report evidence is available to authorized service operators for handling the report.

Sharing a drive and sharing its route are separate choices. A shared route is a redacted copy with endpoint trimming and protected-place removal. Your own raw track remains in your account. Redaction reduces disclosure; it cannot guarantee anonymity or prevent a recipient from inferring information from the rest of your content. Other people may retain screenshots or copies of content already shared with them.

## Service providers and external destinations

- **Supabase** provides backend database, authentication, storage, realtime messaging and server functions. Data is processed to operate these services under the applicable provider arrangements. See [Supabase privacy information](https://supabase.com/privacy).
- **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple, push delivery, maps and operating-system services. Use of those services is also subject to [Apple's privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **eBay:** Only when you choose to open a parts search, your search terms are placed in an external eBay link. That destination may process information under [eBay's privacy notice](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-privacy-notice-privacy-policy?id=4260). A search does not establish fitment or a purchase relationship with Pull Up. Any future affiliate links must be disclosed before opening them.
- **GitHub** hosts this public project, documentation and support tracker. Visiting it or filing an issue is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with a valid legal obligation, protect users or the service, or investigate abuse. We do not authorize service providers to use personal data for unrelated purposes. Provider agreements and safeguards must support the protections described here before production launch.

## Storage, retention and deletion

Account data, garages and saved drives are retained to provide the service until deleted or no longer needed. Raw and pending drive files may remain on your device until successfully synchronized or removed through the app's relevant controls. Signing out is not the same as erasing all account data; account deletion removes account-owned local drive data and generated exports in the app's deletion path.

Use **Feed > your profile icon > Settings > Account > Delete account** to request account deletion. The service deletes account-owned database records and associated stored media through its deletion process. Some shared conversation data may also be removed by that process. This action cannot remove copies another person previously saved outside Pull Up.

The messaging-retention design removes unanswered requests after 30 days, content both participants have cleared, and unsent-message tombstones after 30 days. Closed reports are eligible for removal after one year; unresolved reports can remain while they are handled. These scheduled cleanups require the deployed retention job to run. Operational logs and provider backups can have separate retention and may not disappear immediately with deletion. We do not claim a fixed backup-deletion deadline that has not been established with the deployed providers. Necessary records may also be retained to meet legal obligations or handle abuse.

## Your choices

You can edit profile and audience settings, disable route sharing, change protected places, opt out of leaderboards, control who may message you, change read receipts and push preferences, revoke iOS permissions, remove content using available controls, and delete your account. Revoking a permission does not by itself delete data already stored.

The app re-encodes photos and prepares videos to remove location/device metadata before upload. Visible landmarks and information you type or show can still disclose a location. Do not upload sensitive material you do not intend to share with the chosen audience.

## Children and age

Pull Up is not intended for children under 13. A store age rating is not age verification. Age-related restrictions and regional requirements must be reflected in the app's actual onboarding and controls before public launch. Do not submit a child's personal information through the public support tracker.

## Security

The app uses access controls and operating-system protections, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Authorized operators may access information when needed to operate, secure and support the service or handle reports. Do not send sensitive information assuming messages are end-to-end encrypted.

## Contact and changes

For a general privacy question, [contact the developer through the public support tracker](https://github.com/AaravJit/pull-up/issues/new). Post only a non-sensitive description; do not include credentials, precise locations, private messages or identity documents. Account deletion is available inside the app as described above.

We may update this policy when the service changes and will update the date above. Material changes to collection, use or sharing require corresponding updates to app disclosures and any necessary user choices before the changed processing begins.
